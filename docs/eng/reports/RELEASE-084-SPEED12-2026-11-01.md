- One measured session per profile: TCP **P4**, UDP **600 Mbps**, payload 1200, `-t 10 -O 2`; inner IPv4/IPv6 up/down. The unmeasured FakeTLS attempt is documented below. No VPN TCP P1, UDP300/ceilings, off/prefer, three repeats, latency or soak.
- Documented buffer-aware lab policy temporarily sets `rmem_max=rmem_default=4194304` on both VMs. Live Qeli UDP `rb >= 8388608` (explicit 4 MiB request), iperf UDP receiver `rb >= 4194304`; PID-owned evidence checked in every window, including TCP receiver rb/tb. No wmem, qdisc/offload or CPU/RAM changes. This is not a packaged `.deb` auto-16-MiB test: the lab RX cap is 4 MiB.
- Existing eth1 qdiscs retained: server fq_codel, client fq with flow_limit=100. A 0.5-second observer records CPU/RSS, TUN/qdisc, TCP_INFO, IPv4/IPv6 SNMP and softnet.

The binary/load configs stayed unchanged; completed windows were not rerun or selected again. Total: **96 load + 12 baseline = 108 windows**, in two separate raw roots.
